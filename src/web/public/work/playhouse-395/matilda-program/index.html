Matilda: everything for the program

The program is a half-letter booklet: a letter sheet folded once, 5.5 × 8.5 in pages. The cover is done. Everything below is what you need to lay out the inside pages so they match it and the flyer: the files, the colours, the typeface and sizes, the title art, MTI’s full logo pack for anything else you make, and the rules from our license.

01 · The cover

Finished, at trim size

Place the cover as it is. It is cut to the page with no bleed, so it goes on the page edge to edge.

02 · Colours

Six colours, from the artwork

The flyer and the cover use these and nothing else. The hex values go straight into InDesign or Canva.

#000000BlackThe background. The key art’s own ground, so the art blends into the page.
#80D8FBTitle light blueHeadings, labels, “presents”, day names. On black only.
#FFFFFFWhiteMain text on black: dates, times, venue, prices.
#9FB4C8Muted blue-greyFine print and credits on black.
#335664DividerThin rules between columns. Title light blue at 40% on black.
#272054NavyThe navy behind the title on the website. An accent that also works on white paper.
  • On white pages, set text in black. The light blue is too pale to read on white; use the navy where a colour accent is wanted.

04 · Title art

The Matilda title for the inside pages

The title comes in two versions, one for each ground. Each comes as a TIF for print and a PNG for screens. Use the TIFs in the program: they are 300 dpi, sharp up to 7 in wide. The PNGs are screen resolution and print soft.

06 · Using the Matilda art

The rules from our license with MTI

  • Use the title and key art exactly as supplied: no recolouring, no stretching, nothing cropped into the title, no rearranging the pieces.
  • For anything printed, use the print files: the cover PNG and PDF above, and the pack’s TIFs and EPS files. The pack’s JPGs and PNGs are for screens.
  • The vortex grounds (XA_VORTEX) go only with the title on white, never with the title on black or the grayscale titles, and the title is never larger than the vortex.
  • The silhouette keeps its size and place in the vortex.
  • Grayscale files only when there is no other way, and never in colour work.
  • Photos must be from this production. Nothing from Broadway, the tour, the film or the book.
  • The license sets required author credits for the program’s title page. Ask Jeff for the wording from the license before the title page is final.
  • The art is only for promoting our production: programs, posters, ads and the like. Nothing sold, no merchandise, and don’t pass the files on or post them anywhere else. Any use the style guide doesn’t show needs MTI’s written permission first.

Questions or anything missing: tell Jeff.
