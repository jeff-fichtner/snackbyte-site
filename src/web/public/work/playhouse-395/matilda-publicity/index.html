Matilda: the flyer and the website

The confirmed marketing direction for Matilda: the flyer, the playhouse395.com homepage, and the Matilda page, so that everything someone sees about the show says the same thing and leads to the same place. The direction is set, and tickets are on sale on Zeffy.

How it fits together

→
The homepageleads with a Matilda card: “Show info & tickets”

The Matilda pagedates, times, place, tickets

The QR code skips the homepage and goes straight to the Matilda page.

  • One look everywhere. All three use the show’s official artwork and title from our license, with colours taken from that artwork.
  • The cast list comes off the Matilda page. The page becomes the place to find showtimes and tickets.
  • The venue is Bishop Union High School, named the same way on the flyer and the website.
  • Ticket prices are set: $25 adults, $18 for seniors and students, $10 under 10.
  • Every piece says “Not affiliated with BUSD”, in very small print.
  • Still needed before anything is final: how seating works. Section 4 has the list.
01 · The flyer

Letter size, the poster artwork with all eight performances

This is the flyer. The QR code works now. Scan it and you should land on the Matilda page.

  • Performances: Fridays and Saturdays at 7 PM, Sundays at 2 PM, plus a 2 PM matinee on Saturday, November 21.
  • The printed address is playhouse395.com, because it’s short and easy to type. The homepage will lead with Matilda (section 2). The QR code goes straight to the Matilda page.
02 · The homepage

“See the cast list” becomes “Show info & tickets”

The bar under the headline currently announces the cast. It is replaced by a Matilda card in the show’s colours with one button to the Matilda page. Everything else on the homepage stays the same.

03 · The Matilda page

From cast announcement to the show’s page

playhouse395.com/matilda currently thanks everyone who auditioned, gives the first-rehearsal details, and lists the cast. It becomes the flyer as a web page: the artwork, every performance, the venue, and a tickets section, with the whole page in the poster’s dark colours. The cast list comes off. Cast members get their rehearsal details from the call board.

  • The tickets section shows the prices and a “Buy tickets” button. The button goes to the Zeffy ticket page. Seating and paying at the door get added once they are decided.
