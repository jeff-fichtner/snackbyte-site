What the board decides

The number

Somewhere between the age-model floor and the restored-condition market read. Where in that span, and whether selling to a neighboring school moves it.

Package or split

The console and case can go separately. The research argues against it — the case is what makes this transportable to a school — but that is advice, not a constraint.

Whether to sell

Nothing here argues the Playhouse should part with the console. It documents what parting with it would be worth.

The market read puts this one at $350–500 on the strength of its condition and its preamps. Nothing here reconciles them — the board is seeing the same two numbers the research produced.

Pushing value up

  • 20 channels of GB30 preamps — the strongest value-retention factor on the unit.
  • Restored and flight-cased its whole life, so no wear on the faders or pot shafts.
  • Clean examples are uncommon. The surviving population is mostly beaten-up church, school and community-theater boards.

Pushing value down

  • The analog market is soft. A Behringer XR18 digital mixer is $509 new; an X32 Rack is $699.
  • Anyone equipping a working rig today buys digital. This board's buyer specifically wants analog.
  • Thin trading. Reverb's price guide returned no sold-transaction data for the model at all.

Confidence: moderate. The absence of sold data tells us the model trades rarely, not that it trades cheaply.
